About Us

A trading house you can count on

Samhui Traders LLP is a limited liability partnership engaged in industrial material sourcing and supply. From our base in New Delhi, we source quality materials — including adhesive tapes and specialty consumables — and deliver them to manufacturing and testing clients across India.

As a registered LLP with formal banking and compliance in place, we bring structure, transparency and reliability to every transaction — sourcing, supply and long-term trade partnerships built for industry.
